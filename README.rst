====
QINU
====

| EWWW.. WHY ARE YOU SAYING THAT??
| QINU IS NOT UNIX.....

Do you want a modern operating system?
But... do you hate modern operating systems?

You'll love **QINU**.

QINU is a modern operating system built around designs
that could be considered "old", but are actually pretty
elegant, simple, and modern.
